I develop AI methods that help translate real-world clinical data into better, more age-friendly care.

As an Assistant Professor in the Department of Quantitative and Systems Health Sciences at Dell Medical School, The University of Texas at Austin, I direct the LEAF-AI Lab (Learning Ecosystem for Age-Friendly Care). My work sits at the intersection of natural language processing, clinical informatics, and team science, turning the unstructured clinical notes that make up most of the electronic health record into knowledge that can actually improve care for older adults, catching delirium, functional decline, and other geriatric syndromes that are easy to miss in a chart and hard to study at scale.

None of this happens alone. I've been fortunate to work alongside talented students, clinicians, and collaborators across UT Austin, UTHealth Houston, Mayo Clinic, and national research networks like ENACT, OHNLP, and NIDUS. That collaborative work was recently recognized with an NIH K01 Award and a role as Co-Investigator on a $27.2M NIA-funded national Alzheimer's data initiative (ReCARDO), and I currently serve as Vice Chair of AMIA's NLP Working Group.

I'm grateful to be building this next chapter at Dell Medical School, as UT Austin invests in an AI-native hospital and research campus that's genuinely rethinking what patient-centered, learning healthcare can look like.
