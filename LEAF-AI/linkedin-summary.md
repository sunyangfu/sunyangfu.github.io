I develop AI methods that help translate real-world clinical data into better, more age-friendly care.

As an Assistant Professor at Dell Medical School, The University of Texas at Austin, I direct the LEAF-AI Lab (Learning Ecosystem for Age-Friendly Care), turning unstructured clinical notes into knowledge that helps catch delirium, functional decline, and other geriatric syndromes that are easy to miss and hard to study at scale.

None of this happens alone, and I'm grateful to work alongside great students and collaborators across UT Austin, UTHealth Houston, Mayo Clinic, and national networks like ENACT, OHNLP, and NIDUS.
